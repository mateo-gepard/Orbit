# Threadmap security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to `security@threadmap.app`.

Include the affected URL or feature, reproduction steps, impact, and any safe supporting material. Do not include credentials or personal data in the first message.

## Testing rules

- Test only accounts and data you own or have explicit permission to use.
- Do not perform denial-of-service testing, social engineering, spam, destructive actions, persistence, or high-volume automated scanning.
- Stop testing and report immediately if you encounter another user's data.
- Allow reasonable time for investigation and remediation before disclosure.

Threadmap will make a good-faith effort to acknowledge valid reports and keep reporters informed. This policy does not promise a bounty or payment.

The canonical web policy is available at `https://threadmap.app/security` and discovery metadata at `https://threadmap.app/.well-known/security.txt`.
